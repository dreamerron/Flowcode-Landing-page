Apps

Free apps people built with ForkScape. Install on any phone.

Every app here is a real, working web app. Open it on your phone, tap Add to Home Screen, and it lives there like any other app — no app store, no account, no price. Each one was published in one click from ForkScape and read by a person before it was listed.

Install one

Tap Open on your phone. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: the browser menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen). The app runs in its own window, offline where it can, with nothing to update by hand.

Publish yours — free

In ForkScape, run your app and click Publish in the App Viewer. It takes the files, screenshots the running app, and gives you a link like apps.forkscape.com/a/your-app/ right away. It appears here once we've reviewed it.

Newest first. Every listing shows what the app talks to on the network — most talk to nothing.

How curation works: a publish goes live at its own link at once so its creator can test and share it. Before it shows up on this page, an automated scan lists every domain the app calls and flags eval-style or obfuscated code, and a person reads that report. Apps are plain HTML/CSS/JS served as static files — nothing runs on our servers. Found something wrong? Use Report on the app, or write to support@forkscape.com.
