Questions, answered.

Do I need an API key?

Yes — your own. Bring keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or Grok, or run a local model via Ollama, which is free.

Does my code leave my machine?

No — FLOWCODE is local-first. The only traffic that leaves your machine is your own provider calls, made with your own keys.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like; your local canvases keep working on the Free plan.

What platforms are supported?

Windows, Mac, and Linux.
