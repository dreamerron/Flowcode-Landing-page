Travel, prices, research, plans, drafts, reminders. Hunch searches the live web, does the legwork and comes back with an answer — not a list of links.

Questions

Is it really free?

During the invite-only beta, yes. When we introduce paid plans, early members keep a generous free tier.

Which countries does it work in?

Anywhere WhatsApp works. Hunch is built for a global audience first — the US isn't the default, it's just one more country.

Can it actually buy things for me?

Not yet. It does everything up to the payment: finds the exact option, checks the price is real and current, and hands you the direct link. Payments are on the roadmap.

What about my privacy?

Conversations are used only to answer you. The things Hunch remembers about you are stored per-person and erased instantly with /forget.

Why invite-only?

Quality. Each assistant conversation uses serious AI compute, so we grow member by member instead of degrading for everyone.
