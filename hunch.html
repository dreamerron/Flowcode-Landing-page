Questions

Does it work in group chats?

Hunch lives on your WhatsApp Business number, so it sees every customer conversation with your business — that's where the orders and payments are. WhatsApp's API doesn't let bots join private group chats as a third member yet; the moment it does, Hunch will be there.

Will my customers notice it?

Not unless you want them to. In silent mode Hunch only reads and records. In assist mode it also replies to customers — clearly, politely, in their language.

What exactly does it extract?

Orders, payments, appointments, leads, tasks, expenses and notes — each with customer, amount, currency, due date and status. You can also dictate records yourself: "add expense 50 for packaging".

What about invoices and documents?

Invoices are generated instantly as print-ready A4 pages (save as PDF with one tap). Reports arrive as chat summaries plus the live dashboard. Slide-deck exports are on the roadmap.

Which countries does it support?

Anywhere WhatsApp Business works. Hunch is global-first: every language, every currency, metric by default.
