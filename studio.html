How it works

Plan first. Render second. You approve in between.

Most AI design tools paint a picture and then try to cut it into layers. Studio does it the other way round, so nothing is a pixel that should have been a word.

Say what it is for

“Poster for Northern Nights, 14 Nov, Aurora Hall, every element on its own layer.” Drop a reference to borrow its style; Studio extracts type pairing, palette, grid, texture and mood as editable rules. The reference's pixels are never reused.

Read the plan, see the price

A plan card lists every layer, how it will be made, and what it costs. Text and shapes are free and instant. Only pictures use an image model, and each one is confirmed before it runs. Untick anything.

Build it, then keep going

Layers land on the artboard. Move, resize, retype, recolour by hand, or ask: “headline bigger, sun blue, add a badge.” Every request applies as one undo step with an Undo button right on the message.

Your files, your tools

In and out of the software you already use.

PSD with editable text

Groups, opacity, blend modes and live text carry over. Opens in Photoshop, Affinity Photo, GIMP, Krita and Photopea. Import a PSD and get its layers back, with a fidelity report on anything that had to flatten.

Vector PDF and SVG

Screen PDF with embedded fonts, print PDF with bleed and crop marks, SVG that keeps text as text. Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity Designer, Figma. Preflight checks resolution, minimum text size, bleed, safe zone and ink coverage before you send.

Vector tools and take-apart

Pen, node editing, booleans, text on path, local vectorising with no model call. A flat image splits into colour layers you can restyle. One artboard becomes a whole social series, text pulled inside each platform's safe zone.

Create

Video, the same way: script → storyboard → timeline → render.

Write or generate a script, storyboard it into shots, cut a real timeline with slip, slide and ripple, lay three audio tracks, and render one pass for YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok, each in its own safe zones. One approval covers the whole render, with the cost shown first.

Open Studio

Same download. Two front doors.

Studio is a workspace inside the free ForkScape desktop app: Graphics, Create and Design up front, developer tools tucked away. Your files and settings are shared, and one click brings everything back.

In the app

Settings → Preferences → Appearance → Workspace → Studio. Or tell Om: “studio mode”.

Launch straight into it

Windows: add --studio to the shortcut's Target.

macOS: open -a ForkScape --args --studio

Linux: forkscape --studio

The flag sticks until you pick Full again.

Bring your own model

Studio plans with the chat model you already use and renders pictures through the image provider you pick in Media settings, with list prices shown before every render. Local models work for planning; text and vector layers cost nothing.

Honest note: text, shapes, vectors and layouts are Studio's strength. The pictures themselves come from whichever image model you connect, so their quality is that model's quality. Studio's job is that you never lose control of the rest.
