Questions, answered.

How does upgrading work?

You get a license key by email and paste it into Settings → Plan in the app. Activation is offline — the key is cryptographically signed, so no account, no license server, and no phone-home. Your app never talks to us.

Do I need an API key?

Yes — your own. Bring keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or Grok, drive the CLI agents you already pay for (Claude Code, Codex), or run a local model via Ollama, which is free.

Does my code leave my machine?

No — FORKSCAPE is local-first. The only traffic that leaves your machine is your own provider calls, made with your own keys. Even Enterprise team collaboration runs on a server your team hosts, not ours.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like; your local canvases keep working on the Free plan — nothing is ever locked behind the paywall retroactively.

What's the difference between Pro and Lifetime?

Same features — the only difference is billing. Pro is a subscription (monthly or yearly); Lifetime is one payment, no renewal, ever. Lifetime is a launch-only offer for the first few months; once it retires, new buyers choose Pro monthly or yearly instead — but anyone who already bought Lifetime keeps it, unaffected.

What platforms are supported?

Windows, Mac, and Linux — plus a browser playground to try the canvas before installing anything.
