Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Try before you pay — that's the real policy

The Free plan is the full local core forever, and the browser playground needs no install at all. You should never have to pay to find out whether ForkScape fits.

14-day money-back guarantee

Any first purchase — monthly, yearly, or Lifetime — can be refunded in full within 14 days, no questions asked. Email support@forkscape.com from your purchase email, or reply to your Paddle receipt. Refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 5–10 business days, and the license key is deactivated for future app versions.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancel anytime from the link in any Paddle receipt or renewal email — it takes effect at the end of the paid period, you keep Pro until then, and nothing further is charged. We don't do cancellation flows, retention offers, or dark patterns.

Renewals

Renewal charges are refundable within 14 days of the charge if you meant to cancel and didn't get to it — just ask. Purchases are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record; where local consumer law grants you stronger rights than this policy, those rights apply.
